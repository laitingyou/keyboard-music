# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Disagree on ideas, not on people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, insulting comments, or other unwelcome conduct

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact
**laitingyou@outlook.com**. All reports are reviewed and investigated
promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.
Maintainers who don't follow it themselves may be removed from the project.
